# P1 findings and recommendations

## Snapshot

- 1,200 synthetic leads reached the funnel; 884 qualified (73.7%), 696 were approved (78.7% of qualified), and 562 activated (46.8% of leads).
- Median lead-to-activation time is 12 days; the observed range is 4 to 39 days.
- Paid Search + Micro activated at 36.3%, versus 52.0% for Partner + Micro. This is a prioritisation signal, not proof that channel causes the outcome.
- Referral + Enterprise had the highest visible rate (58.3%) but only 12 leads, so it should not be over-generalised.
- Retention is monthly-grain proxy activity: the 90-day cohorts are generally above 75%, but sample sizes vary by cohort.

## Prioritised interventions

| Priority | Hypothesis | Action | Owner | Success measure | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Paid Search Micro leads need more qualification support | Add a pre-qualification checklist and a 48-hour assisted setup path | Commercial Operations | +5 pp activation rate in next 4-week cohort | Do not lower approval controls |
| P1 | Partner hand-off is faster and more complete | Reuse partner intake fields and create a missing-document reminder at day 2 | Partnerships + Onboarding | -2 days median activation time | Monitor contact rate and complaint rate |
| P2 | Early activity predicts later retention | Trigger a day-14 activation check-in for merchants with zero orders | Customer Success | +3 pp 60-day retained merchant rate | Compare against a holdout cohort |

## Limitations

Data is synthetic, outcomes are observational, and the monthly retention proxy does not identify exact 30/60/90 calendar dates. Small segments should be pooled or confidence intervals added before operational decisions. The dashboard is descriptive and does not make regulated KYC or suspicious-transaction decisions.
